# Changelog

What changed in each release of Floorplan Studio.

## 0.5.0 (2026-10-18)

- Robot lawn mowers: Gardena SILENO and a charging station, mowing like the real thing

## 0.4.9 (2026-10-18)

- Sonos: speakers modelled after the real products
- Linked entity: suggest the entities of the piece's room

## 0.4.8 (2026-10-18)

- Furniture catalog: Sonos under Multimedia, Philips Hue under Lighting
- Furniture catalog: a subcategory per brand
- Furniture catalog: the cameras and HomeBase under Security › Eufy
- Sonos: bond surrounds and a Sub from the soundbar
- Catalog search: a variable name the release scan read as an entity id

## 0.4.2 (2026-10-18)

- 3D light profile: lamp light painted on instead of real lights and shadow maps
- White light by its colour temperature, as the eye sees it
- Light profile: clean spotlight pools, lit doors and windows

## 0.4.1 (2026-10-17)

- 3D: far fewer draw calls (fixed parts merged per material)

## 0.4.0 (2026-10-17)

- Redraw only when something on the plan changes
- Lighter rendering on slower devices
- README: running on slower devices
- Automatic quality from the GPU, or a measurement on the device
- README: how the quality is picked; screenshots always at full quality

## 0.3.1 (2026-10-17)

- Documentation: both floors of the demo house in 3D
- Documentation: screenshots with the demo house's corrected stairs

## 0.3.0 (2026-10-17)

- Dutch translation of the panel, the catalog and the setup
- Surface spot (opbouwspot): tiltable and aimed
- Furniture rotation as a slider
- Tilted spot: the light and its beam start at the lens
- Spot beam from inside the lens; panel updates without a restart
- README: link to the changelog and the releases
